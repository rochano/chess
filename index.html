Built from the Chess Game design sheet: walnut board, sticker icons and the sun and moon turn indicator.

Full rules are in: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material. The computer player searches a few moves ahead with alpha-beta pruning.

Pieces use the Noto Sans Symbols 2 typeface.

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see roughly which country and city players come from. Names you enter and game results are saved to the score sheet.
