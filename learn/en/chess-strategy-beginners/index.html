10 chess strategy tips for beginners

You know how the pieces move but still lose a lot? These ten principles make the biggest difference for beginners, each with a way to practise it in the game right away.

▶ Open the game

Opening

1. Control the centre

The four centre squares (d4, e4, d5, e5) give your pieces the most room. Start with the e- or d-pawn two squares to claim space and open lines for your bishop and queen.

2. Develop knights and bishops first

Within your first six to eight moves, bring every knight and bishop off the back rank. A piece still at home is not helping.

3. Castle early

Castle within the first ten moves. Your king hides behind its pawns and a rook joins the game. Unsure of the rule? See castling.

4. Don't bring the queen out too soon

An early queen gets chased, and every chasing move develops one of your opponent's pieces for free.

5. Don't move the same piece twice without a reason

In the opening every move counts. Three knight moves in a row hand your opponent three free developing moves.

Middlegame

6. Know the piece values

PieceRough value
♙ Pawn1
♘ Knight / ♗ Bishop3
♖ Rook5
♕ Queen9

Add it up before you trade. Giving a bishop (3) for a rook (5) is a good deal.

7. Check, capture, threat: before every move

Ask what your opponent can check, capture and threaten, then ask the same about the move you want to play. Most beginner losses come from skipping this one habit.

8. Don't leave pieces loose

A piece nothing defends is the easiest target on the board. After each move, check that the piece you moved is still protected.

Endgame

9. Ahead? Trade pieces

When you have more material, even trades make the advantage clearer until you have an easy checkmate.

10. Activate your king

Once the queens are gone, the king is safe enough to walk to the centre and escort a pawn to promotion.

FAQ

How should a beginner open a chess game?

Push the e- or d-pawn two squares to control the centre, develop knights and bishops, and castle within the first ten moves.

What are the chess piece values?

Pawn 1, knight and bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9. Use them to judge trades.
