Play chess online free, no sign-up

Open the page and play. No account, no download, on phone, tablet or computer. Here is every way to play and how to start each one.

Three ways to play

A friend onlineCreate a room and send the room code, link or QR code. Your friend joins and the game starts. Neither of you needs an account.
The computerEasy, Normal or Hard, with hints and undo. Good for practising on your own.
Two players, one deviceTake turns on the same screen, great at a table with a friend or your kids.

Play a friend online, step by step

  1. Open rochano.github.io/chess and open the menu.
  2. Under opponent, pick Online, then Create room.
  3. Send the room code or link, or let your friend scan the QR code on your screen.
  4. Your friend opens the link (or taps Join and types the code). The host plays White.

The two devices connect directly. If they cannot connect, make sure both have a good Wi-Fi or mobile signal and create a new room.

Which computer level?

  • Easy plays like a beginner: half its moves are random and it often misses pieces it could take.
  • Normal looks two moves ahead and works out every exchange, so it keeps its pieces safe and punishes loose ones.
  • Hard looks four moves ahead and always plays the best move it finds, thinking for up to two seconds.

Start on Easy and move up after three wins in a row. Hints and undo turn every mistake into a lesson.

Clocks

Play untimed or with 3+2, 5+0 or 10+0. The first number is each side's minutes, the second is seconds added after every move. Beginners do best untimed or on 10+0.

Why there is no sign-up

The game runs entirely in your browser and keeps your progress and settings on your device. Online play only needs a room code, so there is no email or password to enter.

FAQ

Where can I play chess online for free?

At rochano.github.io/chess in any browser on phone or computer, with no account and no download.

How do I play chess with a friend on another device?

Pick Online, create a room and send the code, link or QR code. Your friend joins and the game starts.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes, at three levels (Easy, Normal, Hard), with hints and undo.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It runs in the browser. You can add it to your home screen from the browser menu if you want an icon.
