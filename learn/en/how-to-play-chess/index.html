How to play chess: the rules in 5 minutes

Everything you need to play your first game: the board, how each piece moves, the three special moves, and how a game ends.

▶ Open the game

The board and the goal

Chess is played on an 8 by 8 board with a light square in each player's bottom-right corner. White moves first, then the players take turns. You win by checkmate: attacking the enemy king so that it has no way to escape.

How each piece moves

PieceMoves
♔ KingOne square in any direction. It may never move into check.
♕ QueenAny number of squares in a straight line or diagonal. The strongest piece.
♖ RookAny number of squares along a rank or file.
♗ BishopAny number of squares diagonally, so it stays on one colour all game.
♘ KnightAn L shape: two squares one way, then one to the side. The only piece that jumps over others.
♙ PawnForward one square, or two on its first move. It captures one square diagonally forward.

Three special moves

Castling

The king moves two squares towards a rook, and the rook jumps to the square the king crossed. Allowed only if neither piece has moved, the squares between are empty, and the king is not in check and does not pass through an attacked square.

En passant

If an enemy pawn moves two squares and lands beside your pawn, you may capture it on your very next move as if it had moved only one.

Promotion

A pawn that reaches the last rank becomes a queen, rook, bishop or knight. Almost everyone picks the queen.

Check, checkmate and draws

  • Check: the king is attacked. You must get out of check on your move: move the king, block, or capture the attacker.
  • Checkmate: in check with no way out. The game is over.
  • Draw: stalemate (no legal move but not in check), the same position three times, fifty moves each with no capture or pawn move, or too little material left to mate.

The game knows all of these rules, so it only lets you play legal moves and tells you when the game is drawn.

FAQ

How does each chess piece move?

King one square any way; queen any distance straight or diagonal; rook straight; bishop diagonal; knight in an L and can jump; pawn forward one (two on its first move) and captures diagonally.

What is checkmate?

The king is attacked and has no legal way to escape, so the game ends.
